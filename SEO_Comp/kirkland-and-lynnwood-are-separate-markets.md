**Summary:** Google Local Finder read for both cities (Kirkland 2026-09-17, Lynnwood 2026-09-14).
Of the twenty firms in Kirkland's "landscaping" pack, only **Hart and Sons** and **Alderwood
Landscaping** also appear in Lynnwood's. Kirkland is held by Doan, Classic Landscaping, GoGreen,
Soil and Stone, Fire Sparrow, Noah, Western Landscape & Pavers. Lynnwood is held by JVM, Cooper's,
Acosta, Ballinger, Flamenco, Hart and Sons. Grizzly appears once in each: **Kirkland #12 for
"irrigation system installation", Lynnwood #8 for "sprinkler repair"** — and in Kirkland it is
absent from "sprinkler repair" entirely, the higher-intent sibling query.

**Why it matters:** a single competitor list treated as "the market" will name the wrong firms for
whichever city is being worked on, and a single campaign will target the wrong terms.

**How to apply:** keep two competitor sets. See
`Grizzly_competitor_research/2026-09-17-kirkland-lynnwood/10-kirkland-local-finder.md` for Kirkland
and `07-live-local-finder-rankings.md` in the parent folder for Lynnwood. Linked:
[[ranking-page-shape-is-service-plus-city]].
