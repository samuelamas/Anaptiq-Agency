**Summary:** measured 2026-09-17. `northernwatersinc.com` has **four URLs** in its sitemap (home,
services, specials, contact), `lastmod` 2017–2025, **440 words** on the homepage, homepage title
literally **"NORTHERN WATERS"** with no keywords and no meta description, and a `LocalBusiness`
JSON-LD block with an **empty address and no phone**. It still ranks **#2** for "irrigation repair
lynnwood wa" (Grizzly's home city, Grizzly absent), **#1** for "sprinkler system repair edmonds wa",
and earns **Google sitelinks**. It has **no map-pack presence** in its own city.

What it does have: domain registered **2005**, Wayback snapshots every year since 2006, "SINCE
1985" and WA registration **NORTHWI784RW** on the homepage, four named certifications (BAT,
specialty plumber backflow, low-voltage electrician, pump installer), and citations on Yelp (15),
BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, Checkbook, Birdeye, YellowPages, Superpages, BuildZoom, ZoomInfo.
It writes **"sprinkler" 2–3×** and **"backflow" 11–17×** per page. Grizzly writes **"sprinkler"
zero times** sitewide and "backflow" once.

**Why it matters:** the intuitive read ("their site is simpler, so we should build more pages") is
backwards. Northern Waters wins on entity age, single-service specialisation and the literal search
vocabulary — not on site size. Grizzly already has the asset Northern Waters lacks: a GBP with
5.0/7 reviews.

**How to apply:** treat irrigation as a separate specialist entity, not a service tile. Use the
searcher's word ("sprinkler"), publish licence/cert/insurance proof, and win the map pack Northern
Waters has conceded. See [[ranking-page-shape-is-service-plus-city]] and
[[grizzly-google-index-holds-the-old-site]].
