**Summary:** tested 2026-09-17. Google served **no AI Overview** for any of five commercial queries
(best landscaping kirkland, who repairs sprinklers lynnwood, paver patio cost kirkland, backflow
testing lynnwood, landscaping near me kirkland) — verified by screenshot and an in-page find, not
assumed. Bing Copilot answered **one**: "how much does a paver patio cost in kirkland wa". It cited
woogoro.com, kirklandpavers.com, nailtheprice.com and **stabuiltpavers.com twice** — the only
audited competitor cited in any generative answer, and cited for operational specifics ("access and
slope increase labor", "excavation depth, base compaction and warranty coverage vary widely"),
not for marketing claims.

**Why it matters:** it is easy to over-invest in AEO on the assumption that AI answers are already
eating this market's clicks. They are not, yet. It is also easy to under-invest and be absent when
they arrive.

**How to apply:** the cheap, correct posture is FAQPage + LocalBusiness + Service markup and some
genuine cost content — the one answer that existed was a cost question answered from cost content.
Note also that **Cooper's Landscape 403s ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot** while allowing ChatGPT-User,
so the market's largest content operation (673 URLs) is invisible to two of three answer engines.
Grizzly's robots.txt allows everything, which is already correct.
