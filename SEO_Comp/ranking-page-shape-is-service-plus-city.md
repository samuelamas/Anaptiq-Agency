**Summary:** organic SERPs read 2026-09-17 for four queries across Kirkland and Lynnwood. Every
contractor URL on page one has the same shape — `honelandscape.com/kirkland-wa/landscape-installation/`,
`gogreenlandscapingservices.com/irrigation`, `newportlandscape.com/irrigation-system` (titled
"Irrigation System Services in Kirkland, WA | Sprinkler Repair"), `stabuiltpavers.com/kirkland-patio-paver-installer`,
`cooperslandscapellc.com/irrigation-system-maintenance…`, `leesgeneralandscaping.com/cities/lynwood`,
`classiclandscaping.com/landscaping-kirkland-wa/landscape-design-build/`. One service, one city,
both in the title.

Three of the pages ranking above Grizzly are **exact-match-domain microsites** —
`kirklandsprinklerrepair.com`, `lynnwoodsprinklerrepair.com`, `sprinklersystemwinterizing.com` —
with no brand, no reviews and no map presence. They rank on domain plus page title alone, which
means a real business with a real profile can outrank them by copying the page shape.

**Why it matters:** Grizzly's structure is the inverse — one generic `/irrigation-drainage` hub and
sixteen city pages that each list all six services. Neither shape appears anywhere in the results.

**How to apply:** nine real cities × four services = 36 pages with 36 distinct titles, replacing 16
pages that share one. Put the literal search term in the title, including the word "sprinkler" —
Grizzly's irrigation page currently uses it zero times while Google's own category for the business
is "Lawn sprinkler system contractor". See [[grizzly-city-pages-are-near-duplicates]].
